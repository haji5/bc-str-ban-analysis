Did rents respond?

No detectable effect. The listings removed are too few, relative to the rental stock, for any rent effect to rise above the noise in the price data, and larger forces were moving rents at the same time.

Threats to identification

  • Quebec contamination. Montreal’s post-2023 crackdown is a parallel treatment, so Montreal is excluded from all controls.
  • Ottawa isn’t clean. Its 2022 bylaw roll-out violates parallel trends; Seattle’s archive has no pre-period.
  • Truncated scrapes. Spatially uneven truncation once inflated the Phase-2 estimate threefold; affected snapshots are dropped by rule.
  • Which areas are exempt. Small municipalities within 15 km of a larger one (Metchosin, Highlands) are covered, and Salt Spring Island opted in from Nov 2025. An earlier version treated all three as exempt.
  • Partly treated control. Private rooms fell too; design B and a triple difference bound design A.
  • Measurement changes. Price fields changed in 2026, so nightly prices are not used as an outcome.
